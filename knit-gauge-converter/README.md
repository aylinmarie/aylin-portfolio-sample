# Knit Gauge Converter

A simple tool to help knitters convert gauge measurements and adjust stitch counts when substituting yarns.

## Features

- Convert stitches/rows per inch to stitches/rows per cm (and vice versa)
- Calculate adjusted stitch counts based on your gauge vs. a pattern's gauge
- Simple, browser-based — no installation required

## Usage

Open `index.html` in your browser to use the converter.

### Gauge Conversion
Enter your gauge in stitches or rows per inch to get the equivalent per cm, or vice versa.

### Stitch Count Adjustment
Enter the pattern's gauge and your actual gauge to calculate how many stitches you need to cast on to achieve the same measurement.

## How Knit Gauge Works

Gauge is the number of stitches and rows per unit of measurement (inch or cm) in a knitted swatch. If your gauge differs from the pattern's gauge, you'll need to adjust your stitch counts to get the correct finished size.

**Formula:**
```
Adjusted stitches = (Pattern stitches / Pattern gauge) × Your gauge
```
